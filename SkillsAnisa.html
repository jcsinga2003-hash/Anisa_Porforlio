Dancing

Strong

Expressing creativity and emotion through movement and performance has always been something I enjoy. During my high school days, I was once a dancer, and that experience allowed me to express myself, build confidence, and connect with others through music and movement. Although dancing is one of my talents, I have chosen to set boundaries in expressing this skill in accordance with my Islamic faith and the values I believe in as a Muslim woman. Even though I may no longer express my talent in the same way as before, I still treasure the beautiful memories and lessons that dancing has brought into my life.

Qur'an Reading

Strong

Reading and understanding the Qur'an with proper recitation and interpretation is an important part of my life as a Muslim. It helps me strengthen my faith, deepen my connection with Allah, and gain a better understanding of the teachings and guidance of Islam. Through learning the correct pronunciation, observing the rules of Tajweed, and reflecting on the meanings of the verses, I can appreciate the wisdom and lessons that the Qur'an offers. This practice also guides me in developing good character, patience, kindness, and a stronger sense of responsibility in my daily life. I believe that continuously learning and understanding the Qur'an is a lifelong journey that will help me grow spiritually, strengthen my relationship with Allah, and become a better Muslim woman who strives to live according to the teachings of Islam.

Arabic Writing

Strong

Developing proficiency in writing in Arabic, including proper grammar and writing techniques for Arabic letters, is an important skill that I want to continue improving. Learning how to write Arabic correctly helps me understand the language more deeply and communicate ideas clearly. It requires patience, practice, and attention to detail, especially when learning the correct formation of letters, their connections, and the appropriate use of grammar. As a Muslim, improving my Arabic writing skills is also meaningful because Arabic is the language of the Qur'an, and learning it allows me to become more familiar with the words and expressions used in Islamic teachings. Through continuous learning and practice, I hope to improve my writing accuracy, expand my vocabulary, and develop greater confidence in using Arabic in both my religious studies and daily learning.
